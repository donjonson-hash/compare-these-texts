PRICING

Free. No account, no payment.

Compare Two Texts is free to use on this website and as a Chrome extension. There is no trial, no subscription and no limit on the number of comparisons.

What the free plan includes

Everything the product does today:

  • Compare two texts, two Word documents (DOCX), two text-based PDF files, or two Excel or CSV files side by side.
  • Edit the differences in place and download the result as DOCX, PDF or TXT, or print it.
  • Spreadsheet comparison by an ID column, with changed cells and missing rows highlighted.
  • The Chrome extension with the same engine, working offline.
  • Interface in 51 languages.
  • Encrypted local drafts that let you continue later in the same browser.

Current limits

These limits come from running everything inside the browser, not from a paid tier:

  • Up to 2 MB per file and 5,000 spreadsheet rows per file.
  • PDF files need selectable text. Scanned pages are not read; there is no OCR.
  • Text and table content is compared, not images or layout.

See Help & file guides for the details of each file type.

What we never ask for

  • No account or email address.
  • No card details.
  • No document uploads: comparison and editing run on your device. The website records visit statistics with Google Analytics; the extension has no analytics at all. See Your files & privacy.

How can it be free?

Documents are processed on your device, so there are no servers that read your files and no per-document costs to pass on. The developer maintains the product and this website. This page describes the current terms; if they change, the change is published here first.

Questions

Is there a paid or Pro plan?

No. There is one free version with all features.

Is the Chrome extension free as well?

Yes. It is the same comparison engine packaged for Chrome, and it works without an internet connection. Get the extension.

Can I use it for work or in a company?

Yes. The terms of use apply; no licence purchase is required.

Do larger files cost extra?

No. Files over 2 MB are not supported at any price; split the document or remove embedded images first.
