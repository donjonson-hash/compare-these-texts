Compare two texts and see every difference

Compare Two Texts is a free text difference checker. Paste the original text on the left and the changed version on the right, then select Compare texts. Added, removed and changed words are highlighted side by side, so you can check a revised paragraph, a contract clause or a translated draft in seconds.

How to compare two texts

  1. Paste or upload. Put the first version under A and the second version under B. You can also upload Word (.docx), text-based PDF, TXT, Excel or CSV files up to 2 MB each.
  2. Compare. Select Compare texts. Every difference is highlighted, and Next difference takes you from one change to the next.
  3. Fix and save. Edit the result directly in the comparison, then download it. For Word documents you can keep the wording from either version, passage by passage.

What the text difference checker shows

  • Words and passages that were added, deleted or changed, shown side by side.
  • A list of all changes, so long documents stay easy to review.
  • Changed amounts and percentages in text, Word and PDF comparisons.
  • For spreadsheets, rows matched by an ID or SKU column, with changed values and missing rows.

Private by design

Your texts and files are compared in your browser. They are not uploaded to a server or sent to an AI service, and no account is needed. The website uses Google Analytics to count visits and a few actions, never the content of your texts. The free Chrome extension contains no analytics and works offline. Frequently asked questions

Is Compare Two Texts free?

Yes. The website and the Chrome extension are free to use, with no account and no ads. See what the free plan includes.

Are my texts uploaded anywhere?

No. The comparison runs on your device. Loading the website needs an internet connection and the website sends visit statistics to Google Analytics, but your texts and files stay in your browser.

Can I compare Word, PDF or Excel files?

Yes. Read the guides to compare two Word documents, compare two PDF files, compare two Excel files and compare two CSV files. PDFs need selectable text; scanned pages are not supported.

What is a diff checker?

A diff checker compares two versions of a text and shows exactly what changed between them. Compare Two Texts does this for plain text and for documents, and lets you correct the result instead of only viewing it.

Does it work offline?

The website needs an internet connection to load. The free Chrome extension works offline once it is installed: use Add to Chrome at the top of this page.

    About this comparison
    Values from the selected XLSX sheets or CSV/TSV records are shown. Excel references include original sheets, rows, and cells. Numbers are shown without formatting; simple zero-padding is preserved and dates use ISO format. Formulas require a values-only copy. Formatting and embedded objects are not compared. Items are matched by identifier. Record numbers refer to the source files, with the header as record 1. Only selected fields are highlighted. Units and currencies are not converted.
