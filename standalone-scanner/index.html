YOUR DOCUMENT, CHECKED BY YOU

Scan, check, save.

Open a scan, improve its image, recognize English or Russian text, then correct it beside the original.

All recognition runs on this device. Confirm each page before saving your document. Handwriting usually needs manual correction.

How it works & privacy
  1. Open a PNG, JPEG or PDF. Use Improve image to preview enhancement before recognition.
  2. Choose the document language. Recognize a page or selected area.
  3. Click a marked fragment to correct it. Confirm every page, then open the reviewed document.
  4. Download a corrected PDF, checked text, or the unchanged original.

Visible corrections use printed text on a white background. Original image mode keeps the scan appearance with corrected searchable text. PDF annotations and form fields are excluded from the rendered copy; the original download keeps them.

A confirmed document is encrypted in this extension's local browser storage for 7 days. A review still in progress is not saved until all pages are confirmed. The encryption key stays in the same browser profile; this is not password protection from somebody using that profile. Download files you want to keep.

No account, remote AI service or document uploads. This extension is independent of Compare Two Texts and cannot access its saved work.
