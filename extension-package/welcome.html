A few things to know.

Which files can I compare?

Compare Word documents (.docx), text-based PDFs and plain text (.txt). For Excel (.xlsx), CSV and TSV, choose a sheet, a shared row identifier and the columns to check. Use one header row, no merged cells and values instead of formulas. Each file can be up to 2 MiB.

Images and visual formatting are not compared. DOCX footnotes, endnotes and comments are not imported.

Will my PDF keep its exact layout?

Supported PDF edits can keep the page layout, but not every PDF can be edited this way. Check the preview before saving. Complex documents may offer text-only export. Scanned pages need OCR in another tool first. Interactive PDF form fields and comments are not supported.

Can I edit just one document?

Yes. Choose Edit & export to open a single supported document. You can also find this mode under Edit & convert in the workspace. Available export formats depend on the source document.

How do I return to my saved work?

Open the workspace and choose Continue for a saved comparison, or resume a saved document in the single-document editor. Website and extension drafts are stored separately and do not sync.

Drafts expire after 7 days without opening or saving and are removed when you next open the app. Download important results. To return to this page, choose Getting started in the workspace footer.
