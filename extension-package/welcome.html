A little help getting started

Which documents can I compare?

Compare text files and Word documents (.docx), or compare PDF files with selectable text. To compare Excel files or CSV files, choose the sheet, a shared row identifier and the columns to check.

Scanned PDFs need OCR in another tool first. Images, interactive PDF form fields and comments are not compared. Spreadsheet comparison needs one header row, no merged cells and values instead of formulas.

Can I edit or convert just one document?

Open the workspace and choose Edit & convert. Edit a supported document and choose an available download format. For supported Word DOCX documents, choose PDF and check the preview before downloading. Export formats and layout preservation depend on the source document.

How do I open the extension again?

Open your browser’s Extensions menu and pin Compare Two Texts. Click its toolbar icon to open the workspace. To return here, choose Getting started in the workspace footer.

What happens to my files and saved drafts?

Comparison and editing happen on your device, without uploading your documents. Saved drafts are encrypted in this browser profile and expire after 7 days without opening or saving. Expired drafts are removed when you next open the app.

Download important results. Website and extension drafts are separate and do not sync. Draft encryption is not a password lock; see Your files & privacy for details. The editor interface is in English.
