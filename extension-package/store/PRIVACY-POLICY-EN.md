Privacy policy · Updated October 24, 2026

## Documents are processed on your device

Compare Two Texts (CTT) reads the documents you choose to compare or edit. This includes their names, text, tables and supported images. The app uses that information to show differences, apply your edits and generate downloads. Document contents are not uploaded to the developer, a comparison server or an external AI service.

## What stays in your browser?

The app saves your comparison and your Edit & export document separately in local browser storage, so you can continue after reloading or restarting. Saved work includes the original files, edits, supported undo history and view settings. Saved work expires after 7 days without opening or saving it. Expired work is removed the next time you open the app; CTT cannot erase browser storage while it is closed. Download files you want to keep. Older saved drafts receive a fresh 7-day period when first opened after this update. You can also delete a draft sooner, replace it, or clear the browser data. Private browsing and storage limits can prevent saving.

Use Delete saved work in the app to remove the saved draft. Website and extension drafts are stored separately; delete each where it was created. Clearing the relevant website data or removing the extension also removes its local browser data. Downloaded files remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately.

## No tracking or external AI

The extension does not send document contents, browsing history, analytics or diagnostic telemetry to us. It does not read other tabs or automatically access files on your computer. Processing works offline, and the executable libraries are included in the extension. The Explain changes helper runs locally; it does not send a conversation to an AI provider. We do not sell document data or use it for advertising or model training.

## Website and support links

You need an internet connection to load the website. Its hosting provider receives standard connection information, such as your IP address and the requested page. We do not add advertising trackers. Opening an external website or support link leaves the offline workspace and is subject to that service’s privacy policy.

If you choose to submit feedback through GitHub, GitHub receives what you submit and your account information under its own policy. Public issue reports are visible to others. Do not include confidential documents or personal information; use a small fictional example. We use voluntarily submitted feedback to respond and improve the product.

## Your choices

You choose which files to open and when to download results. On a shared computer, delete saved work when you finish. The website and extension do not sync your drafts. To move work between them, download a document and open it in the other workspace.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information in this extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Questions and changes

Contact the maintainer through GitHub support. We will update this policy when data practices change and disclose material changes in the app before they take effect.
