## Name

Compare Two Texts — Diff Checker & Editor

## Short description

Diff checker: see what changed between two versions of a text or document, edit and save. Your files stay on your device.

## Full description

Compare Two Texts is a free diff checker for Chrome. It places two versions of a text or document side by side and highlights every text difference, so you can see what was added, removed or changed. Everything runs on your device: your files are never uploaded to a server.

WHAT YOU CAN COMPARE
- Compare text: paste two versions into the workspace and see the added, removed and changed words.
- Compare Word documents: open two DOCX files and see what an editor changed, paragraph by paragraph.
- Compare PDF files: check two versions of a contract or report without converting them first. The PDFs must contain selectable text.
- Compare Excel files: match rows by SKU, product code or any other ID and spot changed values and missing rows. CSV files work the same way.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Click the extension icon to open the workspace in a new tab.
2. Paste two texts or choose two files.
3. Review the highlighted differences side by side and move from change to change.
4. Edit the result right in the comparison, then download or print it.

AN EXAMPLE
A supplier sends an updated agreement. Open the old and the new file: the extension shows that the payment term changed from 30 to 45 days and that one clause was removed. Correct the wording in the workspace and download the revised document.

WHY NOT COMPARE TEXT ONLINE?
Many websites that compare documents send them to a server. That is a problem for contracts, price lists, student work and client drafts. This extension is a private alternative: the comparison runs inside your browser, works without an internet connection and needs no account.

WHO IT'S FOR
- Contracts and agreements: review every changed clause, amount and percentage between two versions of a contract.
- Price lists and supplier files: find changed prices and missing products in two spreadsheets.
- Reports, policies and drafts: check what a colleague or an editor changed, even without Microsoft Word.
- Writers, editors and students: check a rewrite against the original without pasting it into a website.

FEATURES
- Side-by-side view with highlighted additions, deletions and changes.
- Edit the text in the comparison workspace instead of switching back to Word.
- Spreadsheet comparison by ID: pick a sheet, match rows by an identifier, choose the columns to check, edit cells and copy a missing row from A to B.
- Amounts & percentages: catch changed prices, totals and percentages.
- Edit & export for a single document: fill printed blanks or add text on a supported PDF.
- Export a Word document to PDF with a preview, including headers, footers and page numbers.
- Saved work: drafts stay in this browser, so you can close the tab and continue later.
- Built-in examples so you can try the workflow before opening your own files.
- Interface in 51 languages.

COMMON QUESTIONS
- Is it free? Yes. There is no account and no payment.
- What is a diff checker? A tool that takes two versions of a text and marks what was added, removed or changed. Here you can also correct the text in place.
- Do I need Microsoft Word? No. Open both DOCX files in the extension; the changed paragraphs appear side by side.
- Does it work offline? Yes. After installation the extension does not need a connection.
- What if the rows in my two spreadsheets are in a different order? Rows are matched by the ID column you choose, not by their position.
- Does it read scanned PDFs? No. There is no OCR.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Documents are processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded for comparison or editing.
- Drafts are saved locally and encrypted with AES-GCM-256. The key stays in your browser profile: this is not a password-protected vault, and downloaded files are not encrypted.
- Drafts expire after seven days without use. Website and extension drafts are stored separately.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Each file can be up to 2 MiB.
- Legacy DOC files are not supported; save them as DOCX first.
- Interactive PDF form fields and comments are not supported.
- Images and formatting are not compared. Complex PDF or Word layouts may export as text only, and Word layout may differ from Microsoft Word.
- DOCX footnotes, endnotes and comments are not imported; move them into the main text of a copy first.
- Spreadsheet comparison needs one header row, no merged cells and values instead of formulas.
