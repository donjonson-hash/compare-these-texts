## Name

Compare Two Texts — Compare and edit

## Short description

Review changes between two documents, edit the text and save the result. Processing takes place on your device.

## Full description

Compare Two Texts helps you review two versions of a document and see what changed. The versions appear side by side, with added, removed and changed text highlighted. Use it when checking a revised contract or reviewing edits to a draft.

Open two supported files or paste your text into the workspace. Review the differences, make corrections and save the result using the available export options. A built-in example lets you try the workflow before opening your own documents.

Comparison and editing take place on your device. Your documents are not uploaded to a server for these operations, and the extension can work without an internet connection.

The comparison checks text, not page layout or images. PDF documents must contain selectable text; scanned pages are not supported. Each input file can be up to 2 MiB. Exported formatting may differ from the original.
